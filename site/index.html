        How to make a Zook

        1. Start with the root Zook and add a Blob.
        2. Stretch the new part and choose two-part movement.
        3. Arrange its movement points into a simple triangle.
        4. Attach the leg to the body, then Copy and Mirror it.
        5. Adjust Movement Cycle, enter Test, and use the configured Test pose key (Space by default) to inspect the Zook.
